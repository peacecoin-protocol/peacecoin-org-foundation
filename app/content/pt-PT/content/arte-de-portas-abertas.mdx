## Overview

After our experience in the Games for Change Latin America Festival, PEACE COIN is now being used at a larger cultural event in Rio de Janeiro, in partnership with One Real.

The "Arte de Portas Abertas" festival, now in its 33rd edition, is one of Rio’s most traditional art events, celebrating Brazilian creativity and community. This year, PEACE COIN is part of new rounds of community-based art exchanges and activities, empowering artists, students, and citizens.
