## Overview

PEACE COIN supports the “Games for Change (G4C) Festival.” G4C is one of the world-famous game events, which leads to the solutions of various social issues through Gamification. We provided “PEACECOIN” Token for the prize winners of Games for Change Latin America Eighth Annual Festival, produced by Mr.Gilson Schwartz, Associate Professor of the University of São Paulo.

Games that attract people in all ages are now being applied to other industries such as education, medical care, and nursing care, leading to the solutions of social issues. PEACE COIN, which has the same purpose as G4C, “How to motivate people and make a society more prosperous,” supports the new possibilities of games.

*Gamification:the application of game design elements and game principles to things other than games.
