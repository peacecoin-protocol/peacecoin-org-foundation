## A.U.S Aims to be a Community where Each Member Shines

### Q. What is A.U.S?

First we initiated the concept of A.U.S (Asterism Unity Space)to express the way of relationship and space.
Under the concept of “Let’s play in a global circulation style!”, we have opened 3 shops:Salon de charmer, a.u.s Tokyo, Salon de a.u.s, as places to express our concept. This time we opened up 2 more shops and we are going to open the members-only studio, B.C.R Tokyo, and a new shop in Shizuoka.
In the first place, we established A.U.S as ”Urban Wilderness”, where each member can shine by expressing freely, or buying comfortable and healthy goods to utilize in their lives.

## Money is Circulation of Thanks; Remind Everyone the True Nature of Money

### Q. What do you want to realize by using the original Community Currency “AUS-Coin”?

For example, it is an extreme case, but when I was told “I don’t have money now but I want this,” I told them “If you really like it, you can get it at an exceptional price” as it happened in my own shop. It was because I wanted to contribute them without any thought of profit! However, many of them didn’t understand what I really meant, deep inside of my thought, and they just thought “How lucky it is!” and soon they forgot what they got from me. It was my sad experience.

These actions and thoughts don’t have shapes and drop out from many people’s minds. If we visualize those actions and thoughts by AUS-Coin, we can trace the past like “At the time, I exchanged it with Ms. A from this kind of thought. So this time I will do like that.” In this way, we can remember them and improve to the next action to circulate.

Firstly, it is very important thing to have increasing people aware that there are people working even though it cannot be seen, in order to circulate money and Thanks. If you become aware of it, you will be able to imagine those people’s feelings.

In the beginning, maybe some people use AUS-Coin because they can get some extra money. But I think they will know, or at least become conscious of other people’s feelings by visualization. I want to try this out.

Thus I want to remind everyone of the nature of money through AUS-Coin, and make it circulate with joy.

## Create Secure Communities All Over the World to Connect with People who Have the Same Sense of Values and Thoughts

### Q. What is the future vision of A.U.S ?

We’d like to make places like A.U.S Tokyo all over the world, and create a secure and comfortable network for everyone to come and go freely.

If you come to those places, you will find people with the same concept and the same money, and you can comfortably share your sense of values and always be yourself.

Also, there should be some people who have the same concept and values with us and taking action somewhere. We can find those people with similar concept and connect smoothly with them.

Eventually, we’d like to stay in the world where we can connect to those people who can communicate deeply with the same sense of values and thoughts, and play joyfully wherever we are.
