## Growing Tea and Growing Forests.

### Gazing into the Future.

The tea garden that attracts visitors from all over the world is “AMANO TEA FACTORY” located in the
Kirei Highlands of Minamata City, Kumamoto Prefecture. Located at an elevation of 600 meters above
sea level, at the headwaters of Minamata River, and with a view of the Kirishima mountain range in the
distance, it is a magnificent view that is said to be a tea garden in the sky.

Mr. Hiroshi Amano has taken over the tea garden, which covers 7 hectares of slope, as the third-
generation representative. As a member of pioneers, his grandfather established the tea garden in

1949. After many twists and turns, Hiroshi switched to cultivation without using any pesticides or
chemical fertilizers. In the past, Minamata City in Kumamoto Prefecture was the site of the Minamata
disease. He did not want to become a victim of the disease or give any more victims to the disease.
That is the reason why he sticks to cultivation without pesticides or chemical fertilizers.
”In order to make good tea, you have to have good mountains.” Mr. Amano not only cultivates his tea
fields, but also pays attention to the mountains and the environment where his fields are located, and manages the forests and mountain villages that are no longer cared for by humans. He is looking for a
better future.
