## Overview

Professor Takuo Maeda at Takasaki University of Commerce Graduate School and Guest Professor Toshiyuki Yasui at Graduate School of SDM (System Design and Management), Keio University have started a quantitative study regarding the relationship between the use of community currencies and subjective well-being*1 under the theme of “Quantitative Verification of the Transition of Subjective Well-being of Local Residents by Circulating Gesell-type*2 Community Currency” in Takasaki City and Tomioka City, Gunma Prefecture as a verification field.

Based on the results of this social experiment, the team of “Takasaki University of Commerce Graduate School × Graduate School of SDM, Keio University × PEACE COIN OÜ” will explore the possibility of developing this project into a joint research project on the circulation of community currency to revitalize local economies and improve subjective well-being using PEACE COIN, one of the Gesell-type community currencies.
*1: A good mental state that includes various evaluations (positive and negative) for all aspects of life and emotional responses to day-to-day events
 2: Currency that depreciates over time

## Paper Coupon Type Community Currency Distributed in Takasaki and Tomioka Area of Gunma Prefecture

Professor Takuo Maeda at Takasaki University of Commerce Graduate School and Guest Professor Toshiyuki Yasui at Graduate School of SDM, Keio University have distributed special maps with two kinds of coupon-type community currencies, whose values change over time, in Takasaki and Tomioka area. They also have been conducting a questionnaire survey regarding the effect of the circulation of Gesell-type community currency on the revitalization of local economies, and the changes in subjective well-being of coupon users by using the Satisfaction With Life Scale.

## Project Details and Future Development

The purpose of this project is to conduct quantitative verification of the effectiveness of a The coupons distributed in the Takasaki/Tomioka area this time were structured in a way that when the coupons were distributed, the participants could choose either a 500 yen coupon that could be used for one week only, or a 250 yen coupon that could be used for two weeks.

Based on the results of this project, Prof. Maeda will research which type of coupon would be more beneficial to the coupon users and lead to economic revitalization, and whether the decrease in currency value (the expiration of coupon) would accelerate the speed of currency circulation. On the other hand, Prof. Yasui will focus on how the use of Gesell-type community currency changes the subjective well-being of coupon users.

In the next phase, they are considering conducting a survey on how the use of community currency using the PEACE COIN wallet application will stimulate local economies and change the subjectibe well-being of local residents. PEACE COIN system is an electronic version of the Gesell-type community currency with its unique algorithm of increment and decrement.

## Details of the Social Experiment

Method:Distribution of maps with discount coupon at cooperating stores and questionnaire survey
Area:Takasaki/Tomioka Area, Gunma Prefecture, Japan
Period:From November 1, 2020 to December 31, 2020
Number of Participating Stores:10 shops in Takasaki, 4 shops in Tomioka
