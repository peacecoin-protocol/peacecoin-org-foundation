## Hair Salon “TO/RE”

TO/RE is a hair salon located in Jingumae, Shibuya, Tokyo, newly opened in 2018. You may see plenty of wooden interior in the salon, which gives you comfortable atmosphere as if you are at home.
You will be able to use PEACE COIN to receive their services.
