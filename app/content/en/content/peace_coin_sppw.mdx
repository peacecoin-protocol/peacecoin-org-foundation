## Overview

Thanks for Mr. Gilson Schwartz, Associate Professor of University of São Paulo, our CEO Abe gave a
presentation about PEACE COIN in “São Paulo Play Week,” the educational event held at University of
São Paulo, Brazil. Among seminars of Fintech Specialists and Social Activists, Abe explained about the mechanism of PEACE COIN, with which we can grow in diversity and realize harmonious society.
(Abe remotely participated from 1:36:00)
