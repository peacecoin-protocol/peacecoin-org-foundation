## Presentation at PERSON OF FIN/SUM 2018

On 28th September, 2018, we participated in <a href="http://www.finsum.jp/" target="_blank">FIN/SUM 2018</a> as a speaker. It was a global start-up event themed FinTech (Financial Technology: Utilizing technology in industries such as banking, payment and finance, etc.) and RegTech (Regulatory Technology: Making solutions for issues related to regulatory compliance using FinTech), hosted by Nikkei Inc. and Financial Services Agency of Japan.

“PERSON OF FIN/SUM 2018” held at FIN/SUM 2018 was an event where 18-minute speeches were given by the founders of startups carefully selected by the FIN/SUM organizer.

It was an opportunity to know and empathize the lives and visions of the founders of startups, and our CEO, Abe talked about the background where PEACE COIN was created.
