## Report of Demonstration Experiment

We conducted a demonstration experiment on 7th July, 2018 to test the hypothesis whether PEACE COIN, which is characterized as “money that increments,” is really useful for vitalization of community economy. And we have released the report of it.

We compared Community Currency using PEACE COIN with Community Currency linked with Japanese yen. Please see the report for details.
