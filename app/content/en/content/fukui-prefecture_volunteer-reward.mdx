Fukui Prefecture is conducting a social experiment that incorporates the essence of paid volunteers into the style of unpaid volunteers, which is currently mainstream. 

Specifically, they issue Electronic Coin for volunteer participants and exchange the saved coins for original goods. 

Hopefully, this system increases the self-initiative and motivation of volunteers, which leads to their sustainability. 

For this social experiment, Electronic Coin of PEACE COIN is adopted and a trial demonstration was held.  8 organizations in Fukui Prefecture participated such as an art event organization, NPOs and so on. 

The social experiment will continue until March 2020.
