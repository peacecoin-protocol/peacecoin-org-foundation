### ④Login

![How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app](/assets/images/content/howto-peace-coin-wallet/6.png)
_How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app_

Tap “Login” button and enter your registered email address and password (at least 8 characters) to Login. 

※If you forgot your password, you can reset password by tapping “Reset Password” button. For details, refer to 12.Reset Password.

### ⑤Check Wallet Address & Receive Token

![How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app](/assets/images/content/howto-peace-coin-wallet/7.png)
_How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app_

### ⑥Send

![How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app](/assets/images/content/howto-peace-coin-wallet/8.png)
_How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app_

Tap “Send” button to send Tokens.

You have 3 ways to select the recipient.

①Tap “Choose Friend” to choose from the list of users you have already made transactions with.

②Tap “Scan QR Code” and scan the recipient’s QR Code. 

③Tap “Specify wallet address” and enter the recipient’s wallet address.

Enter the amount you send. 

You can attach a message (Optional).

Tap “Send” button.

## Ⅲ.Other functions.
### ①Edit Profile

![How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app](/assets/images/content/howto-peace-coin-wallet/12.png)
_How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app_
Tap “My Page” then “Edit Profile” to edit your profile. 

You can enter “Name, Username, Date of Birth, Gender, Country, Postal Code, Address, Biography, Things I can do, and Things I want someone do.”

### ②Display PCE, PCE-NOTE

![How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app](/assets/images/content/howto-peace-coin-wallet/13.png)
_How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app_
Tap “My Page” then “Edit Profile” to edit your profile. 

You can enter “Name, Username, Date of Birth, Gender, Country, Postal Code, Address, Biography, Things I can do, and Things I want someone do.”

Tap the Settings of Blockchain Wallet, and tap either “Scan QR Code” or “Specify wallet address.” 

Regarding Blockchain Wallet Address, refer to 7. Receiveof How to Use PCE Wallet

By entering Blockchain address, theamount of PCE/PCE-NOTE you have and their current price will be displayed.

### ③Reset Password
![How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app](/assets/images/content/howto-peace-coin-wallet/14.png)
_How to use PEACE COIN Wallet app_

Tap “Reset Password,” enter your registered email addressand tap “Send.”

Check your email and enter the 5-digit confirmation code. 

Enter your new password and tap “Update.”

Now you can loginwith the new password.
