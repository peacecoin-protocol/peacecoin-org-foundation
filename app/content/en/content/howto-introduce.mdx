## What can you do with PEACE COIN?

You can create an ORIGINAL CURRENCYbased on PEACE COIN, and you can remit and use the currency (Token) within your community.

You can visualize HUMAN RELATIONSHIPwhich your community aims for.

### 2.Apply for the usage of PEACE COIN Trial Token

PEACE COIN Trial Token is available for those who want to demonstrate PEACE COIN.
Please fill in the application form for the usage of PEACE COIN Trial Token so that we can provide the Token collectively to your community leader.

PEACE COIN Trial Token will be provided to your community leader, then the leader can distribute the Token to community members (using PEACE COIN Wallet App.) so that everyone in the community can try to use it.

<a class="btn btn-secondary is-lg is-nr mlxs fsm" href="https://goo.gl/forms/r6vk1QxeEt5LHpMU2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Apply for PEACE COIN Trial Token from HERE </a>

※You can use PEACE COIN Trial Token for FREE.

※We will provide 500 Token per person.

### 3.Contact us if you are the Community Leader who wants actual introduction of PEACE COIN

For community leaders who want to actually introduce PEACE COIN, such as issuing original currency available for your community, or adding your original currency to PEACE COIN Wallet App., please contact us.
Our customer support team will reply you shortly.

For actual introduction of PEACE COIN, please email us at→ <a href="mailto:info@peace-coin.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">info@peace-coin.org</a>
