## PEACE COIN Introduced The Origin of Money and its Mechanism

On 10th November, 2018, we made a presentation for the Collaboration Seminar with Mr. Junichi Ikebe, who operates the Community Currency “Yorodzu” in Fujino, Sagamihara, Kanagawa, Japan. “Yorodzu” is a Community Currency based on LETS, and already has a number of users in Fujino. They have a heartwarming community with strong relationship, where appreciation circulates.

### Presentation Handouts

We explained mainly about the origin of money and its mechanism in the presentation. And we have released the presentation handouts.
