## Overview

We have released the Community Relations Report 2025, "A New Way of Decision-Making for Communities Built on Trust and Action."

This report provides a clear and comprehensible introduction to the features of PEACE COIN, its future outlook, and use cases of communities that have implemented it.

<a href="https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/community-relations-report-2025_peace-coin_en-pdf/283838347" target="_blank">https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/community-relations-report-2025_peace-coin_en-pdf/283838347</a>

It also refers to a governance model for a complex and evolving society.

Although PEACE COIN is a beta version so far, it is being adopted to communities around the world.

Some of the actual issues that can be solved by implementing and circulating PEACE COIN are also provided in this report.
